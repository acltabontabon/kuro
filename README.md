# KURO

> Knowledge from Unified Real-world Opinions
>
> Transform internet noise into informed perspectives.

## What is KURO?

KURO helps people form better-informed opinions before making important life decisions.

Whether choosing a job, renting a home, moving to a new neighborhood, or selecting a service provider, people often rely on fragmented information scattered across forums, reviews, social media, and online communities.

KURO gathers these public signals and uses AI to identify recurring patterns, themes, sentiment, and community perspectives.

The result is not a definitive answer.

The result is an informed inference.

A KURO.

## Why?

The internet already contains millions of experiences.

People share stories about their employers, apartments, neighborhoods, internet providers, products, and everyday decisions. These experiences often contain valuable signals, but they are fragmented across countless websites, forums, reviews, and discussions.

Most people either spend hours researching or make decisions with incomplete information.

KURO exists to help bridge that gap.

## Philosophy

KURO does not decide what is true.

KURO does not tell users what to do.

Instead, KURO identifies the patterns, themes, and signals that emerge from publicly available experiences and presents them as an informed inference.

The final decision always belongs to the user.

We believe transparency is more valuable than certainty.

Rather than acting as an oracle, KURO helps users understand the collective experiences behind important decisions.

## Initial Focus

The first version of KURO focuses on two of the most common decisions faced by independent adults.

### Employment Intelligence

Understand what employees and candidates are saying before accepting a job offer.

Examples:

- Should I work here?
- What is the culture like?
- What are the common complaints?
- Is career growth realistic?

### Rental Intelligence

Understand what residents and tenants are saying before signing a lease.

Examples:

- Is this building well managed?
- Is the area safe?
- Are there recurring issues?
- What do current residents wish they knew before moving in?

## How It Works

1. Gather public signals from online communities, reviews, forums, and discussions.
2. Identify recurring themes, sentiment, and patterns.
3. Surface supporting evidence and confidence indicators.
4. Produce an informed perspective based on the available signals.

## Long-Term Vision

Over time, KURO may expand into other life decisions, including:

- Financial products
- Internet providers
- Schools and universities
- Healthcare providers
- Consumer purchases
- Cities and neighborhoods
- Services and subscriptions

Wherever people share experiences, there are signals to learn from.

Wherever there are signals, there is a KURO waiting to be formed.
